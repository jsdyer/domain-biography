    Certificate issuers

    What leaves your browser. The domain you enter is sent directly from your browser to IANA (to find the right registry), the registry's RDAP service (for .ca, CIRA's), crt.sh and the Internet Archive. Nothing is sent to coolcode.ca and nothing is stored. RDAP shows only what the registry publishes today, so earlier registrars and owners are not shown unless an event records them. Certificate logs start around 2013, and archive coverage varies.
